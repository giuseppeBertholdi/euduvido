## Description
How many feet tall is the Burj Khalifa? Nobody knows. Everybody swears they do.

I Doubt It! is a party game of bluffing with numbers. One question, one number nobody knows. Each player raises the bid: "it's 1,400 feet or more." You don't need to know, you just need to look like you do. Think someone's making it up? Yell I DOUBT IT! and the answer decides who loses a life.

HOW IT WORKS
• One question, everyone guesses a number
• Each bid has to be higher than the last
• Doubt the bid you don't buy
• Wrong call? Lose a "!". Last one standing wins

PLAY YOUR WAY
• One phone: pass it around the table, 2 to 8 people
• PIN rooms: everyone on their own phone, with trash-talk reactions
• Daily challenge: 5 questions, the same for everyone, share your score like Wordle

500+ QUESTIONS
Animals, the human body, the internet, food, sports, science and space, the 90s & 2000s, memes, music, money, history, around the world, and a spicy pack for the right crowd. Every answer is checked against a public source, with a fun fact to settle the argument.

I DOUBT IT! PRO
The classic pack is free. Pro unlocks every pack, in PIN rooms too. Weekly, monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage them in your iPhone Settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
